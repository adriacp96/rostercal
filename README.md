## 🌟 What is RosterCal?

**RosterCal** is a next-generation calendar sync tool built specifically for **Emirates Cabin Crew**. It seamlessly extracts your schedule from the Emirates Crew Portal and converts it into a clean `.ics` calendar file for **Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, and Outlook**.

No more manually typing in flight sectors, calculating time zone differences for layovers, or guessing standby windows.

---

## ❤️ Why Crew Love It

* 🪄 **Magic Overlay:** Use our smart bookmarklet to overlay RosterCal directly on top of the Crew Portal. One click, and your roster is parsed automatically with a beautiful glassmorphism interface.
* 🔒 **Your Privacy is Guaranteed:** Parsing happens entirely offline within your own browser. Your employee details and flight schedules are **never** uploaded, stored, or sent to any cloud server.
* 🌍 **Pure Math Time Zones:** Built-in intelligence for 145+ Emirates destinations automatically converts local departures, arrivals, and ground times to the correct global formats.
* 🏨 **Auto-Layovers & Turnarounds:** Automatically detects your ground time at outstations and generates dedicated Station Rest / Turnaround events.
* 📡 **Live Flight Tracking:** Embeds clickable FlightRadar24 links directly into your calendar event notes so you can track your incoming aircraft.
* 🏷️ **Smart Formatting:** Customise your calendar titles exactly how you like them (e.g., `✈️ DXB - LHR` vs `✈️ Dubai - London`).

## 🚀 How to Use 

### Method 1: The Magic Roster (Recommended)
The fastest, most seamless way to sync your schedule.

1. **Install:** Go to **[https://adriacp96.github.io/magicroster/](https://adriacp96.github.io/magicroster/)** and drag the **Magic Roster** button into your browser's bookmarks bar.
2. **Open Portal:** Log in to the Emirates Crew Portal and navigate to **Roster & Leave** -> **Print/Email**.
3. **Click the Magic:** Click the `Magic Roster` bookmark in your bookmarks bar. A sleek, transparent interface will instantly overlay your screen, parse your entire schedule, and give you a 1-click download button!

### Method 2: Manual Copy & Paste
1. Log in to the **Emirates Crew Portal**.
2. Go to **Roster & Leave** and click the **Print/Email** button.
3. Select and copy all the text from your schedule.
4. Open **RosterCal**, paste your copied roster into the text box, and click **Parse & Preview Roster**.
5. Click **Download .ics** and open the file on your device to add it to your calendar.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is my data safe? Can anyone else see my roster?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes, 100% safe! RosterCal runs entirely offline within your web browser. When you use the Magic Roster or paste your text, your device processes the code locally without sending a single byte of data across the internet.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I change how my flight titles appear?</b></summary>
<br>
Absolutely! Click the <b>⚙️ Customise</b> button in RosterCal to choose between different naming formats (e.g., IATA codes, full city names, or UTC timings) and toggle 1-hour pre-flight report reminders.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work on my phone or iPad?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes! The Magic Roster bookmarklet works perfectly on mobile browsers like Safari or Chrome. You can also save the RosterCal website directly to your home screen as a standalone app.
</details>
