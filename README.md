# ✈️ RosterCal
**EK Cabin Crew Edition**

A privacy-first, client-side web application designed to seamlessly convert your Emirates Crew Portal roster into an `.ics` calendar file for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, and Outlook.

</div>

---

## ✨ Features

* **Privacy by Design:** All roster parsing is processed locally within your web browser. Your schedule and personal data are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to external servers.
* **Intelligent Duty Recognition:** Built-in support for seamlessly identifying flights, layovers, standby reserves, training courses, and leave/days off.
* **Automated Timezone Management:** Accurately converts local departures, arrivals, and ground times across 145+ global destinations using built-in UTC offset intelligence.
* **Smart Ground Times:** Automatically calculates turnaround times and layover durations to generate dedicated Station Rest events.
* **Rich Event Details:** Calendar events are automatically enriched with aircraft equipment types, mandatory 1-hour eGate check-in times (`REP`), and clickable real-time FlightRadar24 links.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Option 1: The Magic Bookmarklet (Fastest)
1. Visit [https://adriacp96.github.io/magicroster/](https://adriacp96.github.io/magicroster/) and drag the **Magic Roster** button to your browser's bookmarks bar.
2. Log in to the Emirates Crew Portal and navigate to **Roster & Leave** → **Print/Email**.
3. Click the **Magic Roster** bookmark in your browser. RosterCal will instantly overlay on your screen, process your schedule, and provide a 1-click download for your `.ics` file.

### Option 2: Manual Copy & Paste
1. Log in to the Emirates Crew Portal and navigate to **Roster & Leave** → **Print/Email**.
2. Highlight and copy all the text from your displayed schedule.
3. Open RosterCal, paste the text into the input box, and click **Parse & Preview Roster**.
4. Review your schedule and click **Download .ics** to add it to your calendar.
