# ✈️ RosterCal — Emirates Cabin Crew Edition[cite: 7]

A 100% client-side tool to convert your Emirates Crew Portal schedule into an `.ics` calendar file for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, and Outlook[cite: 7].

---

## ⚡ Key Features

* **100% Client-Side Privacy:** Roster data is parsed locally inside your browser and never uploaded to any external server[cite: 2, 7].
* **Smart Timezone Handling:** Automatically handles departure, arrival, and UTC offset conversions across 145+ destinations[cite: 1, 7].
* **Auto Ground Times:** Calculates and generates dedicated Station Rest / Layover and Turnaround duty events[cite: 2, 7].
* **Event Enrichment:** Includes check-in reports (`REP`), aircraft equipment types, and clickable FlightRadar24 tracking links[cite: 2, 7].
* **Duty Code Recognition:** Built-in recognition for standard flights, standbys, training courses, and leave/days off[cite: 4, 7].

---

## 🚀 How to Use

### Method 1: Magic Bookmarklet (Recommended)
1. Add the **Magic Roster** bookmarklet to your bookmarks bar from [https://adriacp96.github.io/magicroster/](https://adriacp96.github.io/magicroster/)[cite: 7].
2. Log in to the Emirates Crew Portal and open **Roster & Leave** → **Print/Email**[cite: 5, 7].
3. Click the **Magic Roster** bookmark to automatically parse your schedule and download your `.ics` file[cite: 7].

### Method 2: Manual Copy & Paste
1. Open **Roster & Leave** → **Print/Email** in the Emirates Crew Portal[cite: 5, 7].
2. Copy the schedule text and paste it into the RosterCal input box[cite: 5, 7].
3. Click **Parse & Preview Roster**, review your events, and click **Download .ics**[cite: 5, 7].
